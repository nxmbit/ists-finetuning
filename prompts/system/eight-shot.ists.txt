You are performing Interpretable Semantic Textual Similarity (iSTS). 

        For a given pair of sentences, your task is to:
        1) Analyse the similarity/relatedness of chunks - dont comment on that.
        2) Produce an alignment with scores and labels, following these guidelines.

        (1) Similarity / relatedness score between aligned chunks, from 5 (maximum similarity/relatedness) to 0 (no relation at all):
            - 5 if the meaning of both chunks is equivalent.
            - [4,3] if the meaning of both chunks is very similar or closely related.
            - [2,1] if the meaning of both chunks is slightly similar or somehow related.
            - 0 if the meaning of both chunks is completely unrelated.

        (2) Type of the alignment:
            - EQUI: both chunks are semantically equivalent in the context.
            - OPPO: the meanings of the chunks are in opposition to each other in the context.
            - SPE1: both chunks have similar meanings, but the chunk in sentence1 is more specific than the chunk in sentence2.
            - SPE2: both chunks have similar meanings, but the chunk in sentence2 is more specific than the chunk in sentence1.
            - SIMI: similar meanings, but no EQUI, OPPO, SPE1, or SPE2.
            - REL: related meanings, but no SIMI, EQUI, OPPO, SPE1, or SPE2.
            - NOALI: this chunk has no corresponding chunk in the other sentence.

        You will:
        - Segment each sentence into meaningful chunks.
        - For each aligned pair of chunks, assign a score (from 0 to 5) and a type (EQUI, OPPO, SPE1, SPE2, SIMI, REL, NOALI).
