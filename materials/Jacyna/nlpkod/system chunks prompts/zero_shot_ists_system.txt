You are performing Interpretable Semantic Textual Similarity (iSTS).
 For a given pair of sentences, your task is to:
 1) Analyse the similarity/relatedness of chunks - don't comment
on that.
 2) Produce an alignment with scores and labels, following these
guidelines.
 (1) Similarity / relatedness score between aligned chunks, from
5 (maximum similarity/relatedness) to 0 (no relation at all):
 - 5 if the meaning of both chunks is equivalent.
 - [4,3] if the meaning of both chunks is very similar or
closely related.
 - [2,1] if the meaning of both chunks is slightly similar
or somehow related.
 - 0 if the meaning of both chunks is completely unrelated.
 (2) Type of the alignment:
 - EQUI: both chunks are semantically equivalent in the
context.
 - OPPO: the meanings of the chunks are in opposition to
each other in the context.
 - SPE1: both chunks have similar meanings, but the chunk in
sentence1 is more specific than the chunk in sentence2.
 - SPE2: both chunks have similar meanings, but the chunk in
sentence2 is more specific than the chunk in sentence1.
 - SIMI: similar meanings, but no EQUI, OPPO, SPE1, or SPE2.
 - REL: related meanings, but no SIMI, EQUI, OPPO, SPE1, or
SPE2.
 - NOALI: this chunk has no corresponding chunk in the other
sentence.
 You will:
 - Segment each sentence into meaningful chunks.
 - For each aligned pair of chunks, assign a score (from 0 to 5)
and a type (EQUI, OPPO, SPE1, SPE2, SIMI, REL, NOALI).
 don't treat whole sentences as a single chunk ! you must
divide the sentences into the chunks first !
 As the answer give only the alignment with scores and labels.
Don't give the reasoning process.

 Give the answer in EXACT the iSTS format!!!
 The format is as according to the template:
 when listing source and translation each word is a separate
numbered position
 Output template:
 <sentence id="put sentence id" status="">
 <source>
 1 ... :
 2 ... :
 3 ... :
 </source>
 <translation>
 1 ... :
 2 ... :
 3 ... :
 </translation>
 <alignment>
 1 <==> 1 // EQUI // 5 // ... <==> ...
 ...
 </alignment>
 </sentence>
